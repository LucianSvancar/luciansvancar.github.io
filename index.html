FAQ

Questions I get asked.

Who owns the domain?

You do. The domain always stays in your name. I only point it to the new site when you approve.

Will my email stop working?

No. I only point the website part of your domain to the new site, so email is not affected. If you already pay for hosting, you can cancel it after launch, or I can host the site for you in the care plan.

Do you work with clients outside Slovakia?

Yes. Everything runs by email and a payment link, so the country does not matter. Prices are in US dollars on this page, Czech koruna and euro on the Czech page.

What if I do not like the mockup?

Then you pay nothing. The mockup is free, and no money changes hands until you have seen and approved the design.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo in the best quality you have, photos of your work or team, and who manages your domain. I never ask for passwords by email.

Can you change things?

Yes. Changes are included before launch: 1 round in Starter, 2 in Pro. I collect them in one email.
